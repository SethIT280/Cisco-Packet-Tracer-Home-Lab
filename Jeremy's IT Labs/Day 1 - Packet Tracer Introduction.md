# Day 1 - Packet Tracer Introduction

## What I Did
- Installed Cisco Packet Tracer
- Got familiar with the interface
- Built a simple topology by adding devices and connecting them with cables

## What I Learned
- A computer network is at least 2 nodes that can share resources
- Different network devices: routers, switches, firewalls, and endpoints
- The difference between clients and servers
  - Clients are nodes that request resources, and servers are nodes that provide them
